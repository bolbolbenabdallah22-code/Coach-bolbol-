SESSION PRINCIPLES

Train smarter

01

Warm up first

Raise body temperature and prepare joints, shoulders, hips and ankles before impact work.

02

Technique before speed

Build accurate movement first, then gradually increase speed and intensity.

03

Control the intensity

Not every round needs maximum effort. Use controlled intensity to maintain quality.

04

Recover between sessions

Sleep, hydration and recovery are part of your training program.

⚠️

Train with control

Use appropriate equipment and controlled technique. Stop if you experience sharp, unusual or worsening pain.
