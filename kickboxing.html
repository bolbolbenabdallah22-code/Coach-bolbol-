Beginner Kickboxing

Build fundamentals, movement and conditioning.

Round structure:
Use controlled technique before increasing speed. For bag and combination work, maintain clean form, breathing and guard position throughout each round.
Coach note:
Warm up before every session. If you experience sharp pain, unusual numbness, dizziness or loss of control, stop the session and seek appropriate professional assessment.
