FAT LOSS STRATEGY

What matters most

🥗

Calorie Control

Fat loss primarily depends on maintaining an appropriate calorie deficit over time.

💪

Resistance Training

Strength training helps maintain muscle and performance while body weight decreases.

🚶

Daily Activity

Walking and general daily movement can increase energy expenditure without excessive training fatigue.

😴

Recovery

Sleep and recovery support training quality, appetite control and long-term consistency.

PROGRESSION

How to approach fat loss

01

Start with a manageable deficit

Avoid unnecessarily aggressive calorie restriction. A sustainable deficit is easier to maintain.

02

Keep strength work consistent

Continue challenging the major muscle groups while adjusting volume according to recovery.

03

Use cardio strategically

Cardio supports energy expenditure, but more is not automatically better.

04

Track the trend

Use repeated weigh-ins and progress measurements instead of judging results from a single day's body weight.

⚠️

Don't chase exhaustion

Fat loss does not require destroying yourself with endless cardio or extreme training. Quality, recovery and consistency come first.
