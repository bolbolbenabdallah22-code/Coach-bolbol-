Beginner Fat Loss

Build consistency while combining strength and conditioning.

Fat-loss principle:
Fat loss is primarily driven by sustained energy balance. Training helps preserve muscle, improve fitness and increase energy expenditure. Progress should be judged over weeks, not from a single day.
Coach note:
Start at an intensity you can recover from. Increase volume or intensity progressively rather than trying to maximize calorie burn every session.
