🔥 Fat Loss Program

Lose body fat while preserving muscle, strength and performance. Progressive training + conditioning + recovery.

Week 1 • Foundation

Build technique, work capacity and consistency. Keep approximately 2–3 reps in reserve on most strength exercises.

⚡ Fat Loss Rules

✓
Progressive overload: increase reps or load gradually while maintaining good technique.
✓
Strength first: resistance training helps preserve lean mass during a calorie deficit.
✓
Cardio intelligently: use moderate cardio and short HIIT sessions without destroying recovery.
✓
Recovery matters: sleep, hydration and rest are part of the program.
✓
Target pace: for many people, around 0.5% bodyweight per week is a reasonable starting target, adjusted according to progress and recovery.
